Conceptual Class Model

  • Seven primary classes: User, Job, Application, Subscription, VerificationRequest, Setting, Payment.
  • A User has many Jobs (as employer), many Applications (as student), many Subscriptions, and may have one VerificationRequest.
  • A Job has many Applications and may have associated Payments.
  • An Application belongs to one User and one Job, with a unique constraint on (jobId, userId).
  • A Setting is a singleton (single row) storing global configuration.
  • Payment is an audit log linking a User, Job, and optionally an Application.
Full class diagram with attributes and operations in Chapter 3, Figure 3.5. Complete Prisma schema in Appendix C.
- Seven classes chosen to mirror Prisma models one-to-one for traceability. - Singleton Setting avoids environment-only config drift across deploys. - Unique constraint on (jobId, userId) enforces one-application-per-student rule. - Payment kept as audit log rather than transactional record (manual review). - Mention that full attribute-level detail lives in the appendix schema.
