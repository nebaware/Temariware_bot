Introduction & Background

  • Telegram is the dominant messaging platform for Ethiopian university students, with millions of daily active users.
  • Informal job channels (Afriwork, Yegna Tutors Harrar) post tutoring, freelance, and part-time roles daily.
  • These channels are free, instant, and reach a large targeted audience.
  • However, posts are unstructured free text with no filtering, no archive, and no verification.
  • Temariware proposes a structured Telegram Mini App + Bot to solve these gaps.
  • Combines the reach of Telegram with the structure of a traditional job-matching platform.
