Significance of the Project

  • For Students: reduces friction of finding part-time work; subscribe to subject/location alerts; apply with one tap using pre-verified profile; mandatory documents build trust with employers.
  • For Employers: structured applicant tracking; per-job application fee filters casual browsers; review documents in one panel; accept/reject with one tap; auto-close positions when hiring target met.
  • For the Ecosystem: reference implementation of a production Telegram Mini App on free-tier infrastructure; demonstrates youth entrepreneurship without upfront capital; formalises a previously informal labour market.
  • Creates data on youth employment patterns for future research.
  • Sustainable revenue model through configurable fees.
  • Multilingual (EN/AM/OR) ensures accessibility across Ethiopia.
Temariware — Senior Project Defense 11
  • Lead with the ecosystem angle — committee responds well to entrepreneurship narratives.
  • Highlight the per-job application fee as both a filter and a sustainability lever.
  • Mention that mandatory documents shift informal-market trust into a verifiable workflow.
  • Multilingual support covers the three largest student populations in Ethiopia.
