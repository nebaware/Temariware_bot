Essential Use Case Model

Four primary actors, fifteen use cases.

Student
Browse Job Feed, View Job Detail, Complete Profile, Apply to Job, Subscribe to Alerts, Upload Payment, View My Applications
Employer
Post Job, Review Applicants, Accept Applicant, Reject Applicant, Verify Employer
Administrator
Approve Job, Approve Payment, Approve Verification, Configure Settings
Telegram Bot
Handle Slash Commands, Push Job Alerts, Broadcast to Channel
Include relationship: Apply to Job includes Check Profile Complete. Extend relationship: Post Job extends Upload Payment Screenshot (only when fee > 0). Full use case diagram in Chapter 2, Figure 2.7.
Temariware — Senior Project Defense 18
  • Lead with the four actors — they map directly to the role field in the User table.
  • The Apply-to-Job path is the most important — it triggers profile checks, application fee, and employer notification.
  • The Post-Job extend relationship is conditional on jobPostFee > 0.
  • The Bot actor is non-human but treated as a primary actor because it owns three use cases.
  • Reference Chapter 2 Figure 2.7 for the full UML use case diagram.
