Statement of the Problem

  • Job posts are unstructured free text — impossible to filter by subject, location, or salary.
  • No persistent archive — opportunities disappear from chat history within hours.
  • Neither employers nor students are verified — scams and no-shows are common.
  • Application process is highly manual — students forward CVs repeatedly, no tracking.
  • No payment mechanism — channels cannot sustain themselves or filter serious applicants.
  • Existing platforms (EthioJobs, Ezega) focus on full-time graduate roles, not student part-time work.
Temariware — Senior Project Defense 4 / 27
  • Walk through each pain point with a concrete example observed in the channels.
  • Example for unstructured posts: a tutor job buried under fifty casual messages.
  • Example for no archive: a student missed a role because chat scrolled past it overnight.
  • Example for verification: a fake employer collected CVs and disappeared.
  • Contrast with EthioJobs/Ezega to show the gap is specifically for student part-time work.
