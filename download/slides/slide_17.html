Business Rules & Constraints

Key Business Rules

  • Only verified employers or admins can post jobs.
  • Students cannot apply until profile is complete (3 documents uploaded).
  • One application per student per job (unique constraint).
  • Job posting requires payment screenshot (default 500 ETB).
  • Application fees are per-job configurable by employer.
  • Auto-close when filledPositions reaches positionsAvailable.

Key Constraints

  • Free-tier infrastructure only (zero recurring cost).
  • Screenshot-based payment (no Chapa API in v1).
  • Images stored as base64 in PostgreSQL (1MB max).
  • Mini App runs inside Telegram WebApp container.
  • No web_app buttons in channel posts (only URL buttons).
  • Three languages with runtime switching.
Temariware — Senior Project Defense 17
  • The "one application per student per job" rule (Prisma unique constraint) shaped the Application table design.
  • The base64 image storage constraint drove the client-side Canvas compression requirement.
  • Telegram's URL-button-only limitation for channel posts shaped the broadcast UX.
  • Free-tier constraint was a hard requirement, not a preference — every architectural decision was filtered through it.
