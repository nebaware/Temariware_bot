Methodology & Approach

  • Object-Oriented Software Development approach (per university guideline).
  • Iterative and incremental development process — each iteration produces a working slice.
  • Loosely based on the Agile Unified Process — lightweight upfront design + continuous integration.

  • Continuous deployment to Vercel staging environment throughout development.
