Current System Description

  • Informal Telegram channels (Afriwork, Yegna Tutors Harrar) broadcast job posts as free text.
  • Employer composes a free-text message and forwards to channel operator's bot.
  • Operator manually reviews, reformats, and publishes to the channel.
  • Interested students forward the post + CV to employer's personal Telegram.
  • Employer reviews applications in private chat, contacts candidates directly.
  • Payments (when required) made via Telebirr with no formal record.
The current "system" is a manual, chat-based workflow — fast and familiar, but with no structure, no archive, and no audit trail. Temariware preserves the speed while adding the missing scaffolding.
Temariware — Senior Project Defense 14
  • Afriwork posts roughly 10–20 jobs/day; Yegna Tutors Harrar focuses on tutoring roles.
  • Channel operators are unpaid volunteers — bottleneck for verification.
  • Telebirr screenshots are the de-facto payment proof today; no shared ledger exists.
  • The "forward CV" pattern leaks personal data to unrelated employers.
  • This baseline motivates every functional requirement in Chapter 2.
